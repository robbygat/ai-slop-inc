The AI Slop
Manifesto.

Most technology arrives top-down: a company declares what comes next, then asks everyone else to reorganize their lives around it.

We build from the bottom up: AI-native products that start with play, creativity, and other people. Familiar enough to try. Good enough to win over someone who currently wants nothing to do with AI.

“AI Slop” is deliberate. The name lowers the temperature and puts us at eye level with the people using the technology. AI should feel less like a mandate and more like something anyone can try on their own terms.

Andy Warhol turned mass culture into Pop Art. We believe it’s our turn to shape the next evolution: “slop art,” made through software.
