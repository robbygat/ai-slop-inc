The Lab

We took the insult and made it our standard.

People call AI-made software and games slop — soulless, disposable noise with no author behind it. We put the word on our incorporation papers anyway. Naming ourselves AI Slop Inc. is a permanent dare: every frame we ship has to look, move, and play so well that the name becomes absurd.

Most AI labs stop at chat boxes, benchmark charts, or ten-second video clips nobody can touch. We are a one-of-a-kind applied AI lab because we build real, interactive worlds — full engines, tactile combat, original art direction, and live software in players’ hands.

Frontier AI collapses the distance from raw conviction to playable build. Instead of bloated multi-year committees, a tiny focused lab can craft monochrome manga horror, sprawling pixel-art galaxies, noir survival streets, and instant mobile worlds — with studio soul at lab velocity.

Why the Name

Claiming the internet’s sharpest criticism forces honesty. When the company is literally called AI Slop Inc., generic output is never an option — craft is the only proof.
