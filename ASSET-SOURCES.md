The Anki Duo images show an application in active development. They are real app captures, not the illustrative store artwork or theme boards. The website does not present them as evidence of a public release.

Device screens preserve their original aspect ratios: 23:50 for Slop and 3:7 for Anki Duo. The Workshop capture is no longer displayed because its source had a status-bar overlap. No AI edits were made to app screenshots.
